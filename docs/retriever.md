Better:


The Retriever is the librarian of your RAG system.

It:

Cuts big documents into small pieces

Turns each piece into a vector (numbers)

Stores them neatly in a vector drawer

When asked a question:

turns the question into numbers

finds the closest matching chunks

returns them

It is the “memory search engine” that the LLM relies on.
